PART 1: THE CORE FRAMEWORK
--------------------------

This model is built on one simple principle:

    Open prices tell you what orders are being matched and filled.

The two key open prices for futures trading are:

    7:00 AM  = Start of New York Session (like 9:30 for equities)
    4:40 AM  = Secondary timing level

When price returns to these open prices, you get information about
what the market makers are doing. Your job is to READ the reaction.


PART 2: THE 7AM / 4:40AM RELATIONSHIP
-------------------------------------

SCENARIO A: 7AM and 4:40AM are CLOSE together
    -> Expect a LARGE EXPANSION away from them
    -> Size up on these trades
    -> Direction determined by first reaction

SCENARIO B: 7AM and 4:40AM are FAR apart
    -> Expect price to trade FROM one TO the other
    -> 7AM to 4:40AM or 4:40AM to 7AM
    -> Scalp between the levels

EXAMPLE:
    7AM at 5000, 4:40AM at 5002 (close)
    -> Expect 50-100 point move away

    7AM at 5000, 4:40AM at 5050 (far)
    -> Trade from 7AM targeting 4:40AM


PART 3: OPEN PRICE REJECTION THEORY
-----------------------------------

This is how you determine bias WITHOUT patterns:

    +-------------------------------------------+
    | PRICE ABOVE OPEN + NOT CLOSING BELOW      |
    | = REJECTION = HIGHER PRICES EXPECTED      |
    +-------------------------------------------+

    +-------------------------------------------+
    | PRICE CLOSES THROUGH OPEN                 |
    | = BREAK = CONTINUATION IN THAT DIRECTION  |
    +-------------------------------------------+

BULLISH SETUP:
    - Price is above 7AM open
    - Price wicks below but does NOT close below
    - This is a REJECTION
    - Look for longs

BEARISH SETUP:
    - Price closes THROUGH 7AM open (closes below)
    - This confirms bearish direction
    - Look for shorts to next level (often 4:40AM)


PART 4: THE ZOOM FRACTAL
------------------------

Between 4:40AM and 7AM, a fractal often forms:

BULLISH ZOOM FRACTAL:

         4:40AM        7AM
            |           |
    High ---+-----------+---
            |   ___     |
            |  /   \    |
    Low  ---+-X-----\---+--- (sweep)
            |        \__|  <- Close back above
            |           |

    - Low gets swept (run below)
    - Price closes back above 4:40AM
    - This signals HIGHER prices

BEARISH ZOOM FRACTAL:
    - High gets swept
    - Price closes back below 4:40AM
    - This signals LOWER prices

KEY INSIGHT:
    "This is not pattern trading. A pattern trader looks at the candle.
    We look at the OPEN PRICES and what's happening there."


PART 5: THE THREE BAR RULE (OLR)
--------------------------------

After an Order Liquidity Run (OLR) sweeps liquidity:

1. Find the THREE BAR HIGH/LOW to the LEFT of the expansion
2. Wait for a CLOSE beyond that three bar point
3. Only then is direction confirmed

BULLISH EXAMPLE:

    Before OLR:
         ___
        |   |
        |___|  <- 3-bar high (LEFT of expansion)

    After OLR sweeps lows:
        Wait for close ABOVE the 3-bar high

    Confirmation = Close above 3-bar high
    Now expect: Retracement, then higher prices


WHY THIS WORKS:
    - Most traders chase after the sweep
    - Smart money waits for the CLOSE confirmation
    - The close proves the market is ready


PART 6: THE RETRACEMENT RULE
----------------------------

After OLR + Close beyond 3-bar:

    ALWAYS EXPECT A RETRACEMENT

Rules of retracements:
    1. Retracements cannot leave the expansion range
    2. Most logical target = Order block
    3. Or the 50% of the order block
    4. Or the open price

WHAT TO DO:
    - Take profits at 3-bar confirmation
    - Re-enter at the retracement
    - Or move to breakeven before retrace

CRITICAL WARNING:
    If the order block is retraced BEFORE taking the 3-bar:
    -> Change in order of delivery
    -> Look for FVG or Dark Pool entry instead


PART 7: THE ORDER BLOCK ENTRY
-----------------------------

Standard Order Block:
    - The down candle at the low (for bullish)
    - The up candle at the high (for bearish)
    - Identified by: first candle that closes beyond a key level

DARK POOL ENTRY (alternative):
    When there's no clear FVG:
    1. Identify the order block candle
    2. Find the first candle whose LOW is ABOVE the OB high
    3. That zone is your Dark Pool entry
    4. Price can retrace there and still be bullish


PART 8: SESSION TIMING RULES
----------------------------

TRADEABLE WINDOWS:
    - 7:00 AM to 11:40 AM (primary)
    - After 9:20 (equities open) - watch for retest of 7AM
    - After 9:30 (market open) - fresh OLRs

AVOID:
    - PM Session (after noon)
    - "Stars have to align for me to trade PM"
    - "I know where I lose money and where I make money"

WHY 7AM TO 4:40AM MATTERS:
    - Between these times, fractals print
    - After 8:00, price often moves from 7AM toward 4:40AM
    - Use 9:20 and 9:30 for confirmation

PART 10: COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID
---------------------------------

1. FAMOUS LAST WORDS
   "You know what you expect. It doesn't print. You trade anyway."
   -> If confirmation doesn't print, DO NOT TRADE
   -> Your expectation is no greater than what works

2. CHASING THE SWEEP
   -> Don't enter on the sweep itself
   -> Wait for the 3-bar close
   -> THEN expect the retrace

3. HOLDING THROUGH RETRACEMENT
   -> After OLR confirmation, take profits
   -> Or move to breakeven immediately
   -> 20-30 points = BE your trade

4. TRADING PM SESSION
   -> After noon, rules get overridden
   -> "I go quiet. Radio silence. I'm done."
   -> Stay in your profitable window

5. PATTERN TRADING VS LOGIC
   -> Patterns without timing = gambling
   -> Use OPEN PRICES as the foundation
   -> Then patterns confirm

6. IGNORING THE FRACTAL
   -> If both 7AM and 4:40AM inside a range
   -> The range itself is the fractal
   -> First side swept = opposite side is target
