TCM ORDER FULFILLMENT TIPS - STUDY GUIDE
========================================

Source: Order-Fufilment-Tips.mp4 (81 minutes)
Focus: Book-building process and reading matched vs unmatched orders


PART 1: THE CORE FRAMEWORK
--------------------------

The Book Metaphor

Think of all matched orders as a bag of marbles. Each marble represents
a matched buy+sell pair. The bag contains all the liquidity at that price
level. When price returns to that zone, it's "digging into the bag" to
fill orders. Any unfilled orders remain as future liquidity targets.


The Order Lifecycle (4 Steps)

    SUBMISSION --> MATCHING --> FILLING --> DISTRIBUTION

    1. Submission: Orders placed as "lines" - no liquidity exists yet
    2. Matching: Counterparty found, order goes on the book, liquidity created
    3. Filling: Time-based execution (entities must be present to fill)
    4. Distribution: Orders settled, profits and losses realized


Critical Insight

    Submitted order + Matched counterparty = Liquidity

    An order placed does NOT equal liquidity.
    An order matched DOES equal liquidity.
    If price never trades to a submitted level during the matching window,
    there is NO liquidity at that level.


PART 2: THE BOOK BUILDING PROCESS
---------------------------------

Step 1: Find the Book

The "book" is the overlap between two zones:
    - Submission Range: 12:40 PM to 3:20 PM ET
    - Matching Window: Asian session

Draw both boxes on your chart. Where they overlap is the book.
This is the FIRST thing to identify on any chart.


Step 2: Identify Levels from the Book

From the overlap zone, extract three levels:
    - HIGH: Highest matched order
    - LOW: Lowest matched order
    - EQ: 50% midpoint (the average order)


Step 3: Determine Liquidity Type

If orders matched and price moved HIGHER after:
    - Buyers are in profit
    - They protect with sell stops BELOW
    - Therefore, sell-side liquidity rests below the matched zone

If orders matched and price moved LOWER after:
    - Sellers are in profit
    - They protect with buy stops ABOVE
    - Therefore, buy-side liquidity rests above the matched zone


The Bias Formula

    Key Level + Delivery at Key Level = BIAS

    Delivery types:
    - SWEEP (no FVG created) = Reversal expected
    - RUN (FVG stays open) = Continuation expected


PART 3: UNMATCHED ORDERS (GAP DAYS)
-----------------------------------

When the matching window does NOT overlap with the submission range,
you have a gap. Orders in that gap remain unfilled.

What happens next:
    1. Market will need to return to fill those orders
    2. Creates the pattern: run up, match, fill, run down

Example flow:
    Price gaps up
    --> Creates matching above SR
    --> Must pull back to fill SR levels
    --> Then continues to original objective


PART 4: THE 3-STEP DAILY ANALYSIS
---------------------------------

When you open any chart, do this:

Step 1: Find the Book
    - Draw the Submission Range box
    - Draw the Matching Window box
    - Identify where they overlap

Step 2: Check the Fill Window
    - Extend the book levels into the NY session
    - Mark the High, Low, and EQ from the overlap

Step 3: Determine NY Bias
    Ask two questions:
    1. Where is liquidity? (based on how price moved away from matched levels)
    2. Is it logical for the market to turn here? (based on objectives)


PART 5: PRACTICAL ENTRY RULES
-----------------------------

Close Confirmation Rule

Before taking a reversal trade:
    1. Wait for price to sweep the high or low
    2. You MUST see a close above the swept high (for longs)
       or below the swept low (for shorts)
    3. If no close happens, expect continuation instead


The "Don't Candle" Rule

If price sweeps a level but does NOT close above or below:
    1. Wait for a "don't candle" (a pause or rejection candle)
    2. Only enter if the close comes AFTER the don't candle
    3. No close = no trade


Continuation Must End First

    "Continuations must end before reversals take place."

If price is expanding through a level:
    1. Wait until the expansion objective is reached (deviation level)
    2. OR wait until a candle shows expansion is over (failed expansion)
    3. If BOTH are happening together, do not trade


PART 6: DEVIATION LEVELS
------------------------

How to find deviation targets from unmatched orders:

    1. Take the Submission Range box
    2. Copy it
    3. Place 50% of the box at the low of the gap = 1 STD down target
    4. Place another copy above = 1 STD up target
    5. These levels become key zones for deviation trades


PART 7: DLP INSIGHT
-------------------

How Designated Liquidity Providers make money:

    1. Paid commission for every X orders filled
    2. Hedge by building short-term and long-term books
    3. Goal: Commissions + Exit Profits - Losses > Target percentage

Key understanding: They do not close same-day. They build positions
over time. This explains why markets revisit levels.

Entry Checklist:

    [ ] Close confirmation received (for reversals)
    [ ] No active expansion underway

PART 10: COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID
---------------------------------

1. Trading before continuation ends
   - Wait for expansion to complete before looking for reversals

2. Entering without close confirmation
   - A sweep alone is not enough; need the close

3. Ignoring the gap
   - If no overlap, those levels must be filled eventually

4. Overthinking during slow action
   - The more you stare, the more you see patterns that aren't there

5. Justifying losses with stories
   - Rules-based decisions need no justification
